Deprecation Notice:
The Marketing Version 202510 (Marketing October 2025) will be sunset on October 15, 2026. We recommend that you migrate to the latest versioned APIs to avoid disruptions. For information on all the supported versions, refer to the migrations documentation.
If you haven’t yet migrated and have questions, submit a request on the LinkedIn Developer Support Portal .
There will be periodic changes to LinkedIn Marketing Developer Platform APIs that require updating your application.
