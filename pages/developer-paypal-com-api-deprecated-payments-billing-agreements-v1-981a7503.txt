billing-agreements
10 endpoints
POST
Create agreement
/billing-agreements
Creates a billing agreement.
POST
Execute agreement
/billing-agreements/{payment_token}/agreement-execute
Executes a billing agreement, by ID, after customer approval.
GET
Show agreement details
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}
Shows details for a billing agreement, by ID.
PATCH
Update agreement
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}
Updates details of a billing agreement, by ID.
POST
Suspend agreement
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/suspend
Suspends a billing agreement, by ID.
POST
Reactivate agreement
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/re-activate
Reactivates a suspended billing agreement, by ID.
POST
Cancel agreement
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/cancel
Cancels a billing agreement, by ID.
POST
Bill agreement balance
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/bill-balance
Bills the balance for an agreement, by ID.
POST
Set agreement balance
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/set-balance
Sets the balance for an agreement, by ID.
GET
List agreement transactions
/billing-agreements/{agreement_id}/transactions
Lists transactions for an agreement, by ID.
