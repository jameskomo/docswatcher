Conversation Intelligence
Twilio Conversations now supports AI agent supervision
You can now use Twilio Conversations to supervise your AI agents. This public beta adds two Conversation Intelligence operators that check whether your AI agents resolve customer requests and give accurate information, whichever model or vendor powers them. Conversation Insights shows the results across all your conversations.
This release includes:
Task Completion operator : Labels each conversation as resolved, unresolved, escalated, or declined by the agent, and gives a one-sentence reason. It only evaluates the first task.
Hallucination Detection operator : Checks the agent’s statements against your Enterprise Knowledge and the conversation itself, and returns the ones it flags. Claims it can’t check are listed as context gaps.
Conversation Insights support : Aggregate the results to spot recurring patterns and track quality over time. You can also send them to a data warehouse, such as Snowflake or Databricks, with Twilio Destinations.
Both operators run at the end of any conversation routed through Conversation Orchestrator. They use a large language model as a judge to evaluate the interaction.
Read the blog post for the full story, or get started with our docs .

GA
WhatsApp Max Price is now GA
We are excited to announce General Availability of Max Price for WhatsApp marketing messages. Max Price allows you to bid the highest rate you’re willing to pay for Marketing templates, so Meta charges at or below that rate for each delivered message. You can keep your bid at your current rate, bid lower during quieter periods, or bid higher during peak seasons. Max Price is optional, so you have the flexibility to run it alongside your existing fixed-rate messaging.
Max Price requires acceptance of Meta’s Marketing Messages API Terms of Service. At launch, Max Price is available for Twilio accounts and WhatsApp Business Accounts billed in USD. Support for additional currencies will follow at a later date. To learn more, see WhatsApp Max Price .

Messaging
Sticky Sender is now supported for WhatsApp in Twilio Messaging Services
We are excited to announce that Sticky Sender now applies to WhatsApp messages sent through Twilio Messaging Services. If Sticky Sender is turned on for your Messaging Service, it will now apply to WhatsApp. If a sender was previously used to message a recipient on WhatsApp, the same sender will be used for subsequent WhatsApp messages to that recipient.
Sticky Sender is optional. If you do not want Sticky Sender applied to WhatsApp, you can disable Sticky Sender on the Messaging Service. You can also use a separate Messaging Service dedicated to WhatsApp that does not have Sticky Sender turned on. To learn more, see Sticky Sender .

Nov 17, 2026
New Features for Conversation Insights (Beta)
Conversation Insights (public beta) enables you to measure what's happening across all your Twilio Conversations: aggregate signals from every interaction, learn what works, and feed it back so each conversation can improve the next. The following features are now available:
1. Custom measures : Make the conversational signals that matter to your business available in Conversation Insights. Register a numeric value from your own Conversation Intelligence operator, such as a lead score or agent rating, and explore aggregate patterns and trends across conversations.
2. Conversation Summary : A new gold-layer cube with one unified row per Conversation, combining channel metadata, participant info, and operator results into a single analytics-ready record.
3. Data Destinations support (private beta): Build reports and dashboards in the tools your team already works in. Sync the Conversation Summary record to your existing warehouse, BI system, or analytics tool (18 destinations currently available), and join it with the business data that already exists there.
4. Asynchronous queries : Submit long-running or large analytics queries, then poll for status and retrieve the results asynchronously, so intensive analytics jobs don't time out.

Twilio SendGrid Platform
New filters and text search in Email Logs
Email Logs now lets you filter messages by sender, sending IP, event and API key, in both the Twilio Console and the Twilio SendGrid Console. You can also search subjects and server responses for the words you enter, in any order. Filters combine, so you can set Status to Bounced and add an API key to see which messages from one integration bounced. The new filters and search also work in the Email Logs API.
To learn more, see Email Logs in the Twilio Email docs and the Twilio SendGrid docs , and the Email Logs API reference .
