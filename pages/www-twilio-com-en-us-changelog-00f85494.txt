GA
Twilio Email
Multiple IP Purchase and IP Overflow Logic
Customers can now purchase up to 5 dedicated IP addresses on Twilio Email, allowing high volume organizations to streamline and isolate email streams that serve their operational, strategic, and deliverability goals. Customers can segment different types of traffic, manage multiple brands or domains, and scale high volume sending with multiple IP addresses. Fallback options can be configured both on the account and the subaccount level to other IPs on the account or directly to the shared pools (limit of 1M per month).
To learn more about how to manage your email reputation and IP warmup, see the Twilio Docs .

Twilio SendGrid Platform
Email Insights is now Generally Available
Email Insights gives senders near real-time visibility into deliverability and engagement through three prebuilt dashboards: Overview, Delivery Errors, and Audience Health. Filter by domain, IP pool, template, category and more, then drill into message-level detail in Email Logs. Included at no additional cost in both the Twilio Console and the SendGrid Console, with about two months of history at launch, starting August 20, 2026, and growing over time. Your existing Stats pages and APIs are unchanged.
To learn more, see Email Insights in the Twilio Email docs and the Twilio SendGrid docs .

Oct 16, 2026
GA
Marketplace
Unified Login for SendGrid Marketplace Customers
SendGrid marketplace customers can now take advantage of the Unified Login feature that lets you create a Twilio login for every individual user accessing your SendGrid account. This provides a more secure access pattern with the ability to define permissions for each user instead of a single, shared admin permission set.
When accessing your SendGrid marketplace account from one of the marketplace integrations - Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Heroku, or Amazon Web Services - you will be prompted to provide an email address to create your Twilio login. If you already use another Twilio product and have an existing Twilio login, you can use that email address and password for SendGrid access.
This will be rolled out over the next couple weeks for all SendGrid marketplace customers. For more details, see the documentation here .
