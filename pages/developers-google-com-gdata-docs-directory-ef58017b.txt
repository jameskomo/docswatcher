outlined_flag
Many of the Google APIs listed that used the Google Data Protocol are deprecated, obsolete, or have been replaced by newer APIs.
Several APIs implementing or previously implementing the Google Data Protocol have been shut down with no direct replacement.
Some listed Google Data APIs have been replaced by newer Google APIs that do not utilize the Google Data Protocol.
Warning : Several of the APIs listed on this page are
deprecated or obsolete, and some have been replaced with Google APIs that do
not use the Google Data Protocol.
The following Google services provide APIs that implement, or used to
implement, the Google Data (GData) Protocol.
